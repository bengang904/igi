How to play
MoveThe stick on the left. Push forward and slide your thumb up onto the runner to lock running; tap the stick to stop. WALK: tap to walk, tap again to run.
AimSwipe anywhere in the middle. Hold either FIRE and drag to aim while shooting.
ScopeTap SCOPE to snap the sniper scope or binoculars to 4× and back; + / − fine-tune.
Crouch / jump / peekAround FIRE. Tap CROUCH to crouch, tap again to stand. There is no prone in this game.
SettingsSensitivity, scope zoom, gyroscope, and Edit layout to drag the buttons where you like. MENU pauses; tap the menu items as usual.
